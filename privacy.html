Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 28, 2026

Overview

Syncra is designed around data minimization. We collect and process only what is needed to connect accounts and display balances and transactions. Syncra does not sell personal information.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use Syncra, we may process:

  • Financial data (via Plaid): account balances, account metadata, and transactions needed for display.
  • Technical data: basic diagnostics (e.g., request IDs, error types) to keep the service working.

Bank Credentials

Syncra does not collect, see, or store your bank username or password. Authentication occurs through Plaid Link.

How Syncra Uses Plaid

Syncra uses Plaid to connect to financial institutions and retrieve balances and transactions. Syncra receives tokens from Plaid that allow the backend to request data from Plaid on your behalf.

  • Tokens: tokens are treated as sensitive and are not exposed in logs or analytics.
  • Data access: Syncra requests only what is needed to provide the app experience.

Data Storage and Retention

  • Financial data: retrieved on-demand and not stored in a persistent database beyond immediate session needs.
  • Plaid access tokens: retained only while your account remains linked to support on-demand retrieval.
  • Operational logs: retained up to 30 days and scrubbed to avoid storing tokens or raw financial payloads.

Sharing

Syncra shares information only as needed to provide the service:

  • Plaid: for account connection and data retrieval.
  • Hosting provider: to operate backend infrastructure.

Syncra does not sell personal information.

Security

  • HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) for communications between the app, backend, and Plaid
  • MFA required for administrative portals (GitHub, hosting, Plaid, Apple)
  • Secrets stored as environment variables (not committed to source control)

Your Choices

  • Unlink: You can unlink your account in Syncra settings to stop data access.
  • Deletion requests: You may request deletion by contacting support.

Contact

Questions? Contact: support@syncra.app

Syncra may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
