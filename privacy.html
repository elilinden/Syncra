Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 28, 2026

Overview

Syncra is built on a foundation of data minimization and hardware-level security. We collect and process only the information necessary to display your financial data securely. Syncra does not sell personal information.

Information We Process

Depending on your usage, Syncra processes the following:

  • Financial data (via Plaid): Account balances, merchant names, and transaction history required for the dashboard.
  • Biometric Metadata: When using FaceID or TouchID, verification is performed locally by the device hardware; Syncra does not see or store your biometric templates.
  • Technical data: Basic diagnostics and error types are processed locally or in secure backend logs to maintain service integrity.

Security and Biometrics

Syncra utilizes Apple's most secure hardware features to protect your financial clarity:

  • Biometric Verification: The app requires FaceID or TouchID to unlock, ensuring only you can view your data.
  • Background Protection: The application automatically re-locks whenever it enters the background.
  • Hardware Encryption: Your "linked" status and sensitive flags are stored in the iOS Keychain, which uses hardware-backed encryption.

Bank Credentials

Syncra does not collect, see, or store your bank username or password. Authentication occurs directly through Plaid Link, and Syncra only manages secure, scoped tokens.

Data Storage and Retention

  • Financial data: Retrieved on-demand via the backend and is not persisted in a long-term database beyond immediate session requirements.
  • Access Tokens: Securely managed server-side to facilitate on-demand data retrieval.
  • Secure Disposal: Unlinking your account clears the iOS Keychain and triggers token revocation on the backend.

Network Security

All communications between the Syncra app, the backend, and Plaid are strictly enforced over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Syncra rejects all insecure connections to ensure bank-level security for data in transit.

Your Choices

  • Unlink & Wipe: You can use the "Unlink Account" feature in Settings to revoke data access and clear all local secure storage.
  • Access Requests: You may contact support at any time to request an export or deletion of any associated metadata.

Contact

Questions regarding our security practices? Contact: support@syncra.app

Syncra may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
