FAQ

Does Syncra store my bank username/password?

No. Bank authentication happens through Plaid Link. Syncra does not collect or store bank login credentials.

Do you sell my data?

No. Syncra does not sell personal information.

How do I unlink an account?

Open the app settings and choose “Unlink Account.” Syncra clears local caches and deletes server-side tokens used to access data.
