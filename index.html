Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 28, 2026

Syncra respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection

We use Plaid to connect to your financial institutions. Syncra does not store your bank usernames or passwords. We only access transaction data and account balances with your explicit permission.

2. Security

All data is transmitted via encrypted TLS 1.2+ channels. We utilize device-level biometrics (FaceID/TouchID) to ensure only you can access your data within the app.

3. Data Retention

We do not sell your data. Transaction information is retrieved ephemerally and is only stored as long as necessary to provide the service.
