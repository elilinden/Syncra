Common fixes

  • Update to the latest Syncra version
  • Force close and reopen the app
  • Check your network connection
  • Try unlinking and relinking the account (if connection errors persist)

FAQ

Why can’t I connect my bank?

Some institutions block connections temporarily or require additional verification. Try again later, ensure your institution supports Plaid, or contact support with the institution name.

My balances/transactions look outdated

Pull-to-refresh (if available) or reopen the app. Some institutions update data on a delay. If the issue persists, unlink and relink the account.

How do I unlink an account?

Go to Syncra Settings → “Unlink Account.” This clears local cache and deletes server-side tokens used for retrieval.

How do I request account deletion?

Email support@syncra.app from the email you use with Syncra (if applicable). We’ll process deletion within our stated timeframe.
